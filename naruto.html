Shukaku

Shukaku, also known as the One-Tail.It takes the form of a massive tanuki (raccoon dog) made of sand and has a fierce, wild personality. Shukaku was originally sealed within Gaara of the Hidden Sand Village.

Matatabi

Matatabi, also known as the Two-Tails. It has the appearance of a fiery blue cat with two tails and is inspired by the Japanese mythical nekomata. Matatabi is graceful, intelligent, and polite compared to many of the other tailed beasts.

Isobu

Isobu is a giant turtle-like beast with three tails and a spiked shell. It is associated with Water Release and can create powerful shockwaves and coral-based attacks. Its jinchūriki was Yagura, the Fourth Mizukage of the Hidden Mist Village.

Son Goku

Son Gokū resembles a giant red-furred ape with four tails and is named after the Monkey King from Chinese mythology. It uses Lava Release and is known for its strength and pride. Its jinchūriki was Rōshi of the Hidden Stone Village.

Kokuo

Kokuō has the body of a white horse with a dolphin-like head and five tails. It uses Boil Release to enhance physical attacks with intense steam pressure. Its jinchūriki was Han from the Hidden Stone Village.

Saiken

Saiken is a slug-like creature with six tails and a gelatinous body. It can use Water Release and corrosive slime-based techniques. Its jinchūriki was Utakata from the Hidden Mist Village.

Chomei

Chōmei resembles a giant beetle with seven wings/tails and is associated with flight and insect-based abilities. It can emit blinding light and use wind-style attacks. Its jinchūriki was Fū from the Hidden Waterfall Village.

Gyuki

Gyūki is a massive ox-like creature with eight octopus tentacles as tails. It specializes in physical power and ink-based attacks. Its jinchūriki was Killer Bee from the Hidden Cloud Village, who had a strong bond with it.

kurama

Kurama, the Nine-Tails, is a gigantic fox with immense chakra and destructive power. It was sealed inside Naruto Uzumaki, who eventually forms a deep bond with it. Known for its raw strength and powerful chakra blasts.
